# Abstract

In a randomized controlled trial of 400 adults, the Lumora tablet reduced migraine frequency by 30% under supervised dosing. The tablet did not reduce migraine frequency in children.

# Discussion

The tablet may reduce migraine frequency in adults, but only under supervised dosing.
